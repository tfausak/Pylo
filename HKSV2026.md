# Native 4K Secure Video recording

Enable **4K Secure Video Recording** in Pylo's camera settings and select
**Stream and Record** in Home. Enable Pylo's microphone and Home's **Record
Audio** option for sound.

The option defaults off. It requires iOS 17.4+/macOS 14+, a selected camera
that supports the native 4K capture preset and 3840×2160 at 30 fps, and a Home
hub capable of receiving this recording path. Unsupported cameras keep the
existing recording path; smaller frames are never upscaled and called 4K.

## Behavior

Recording uses HEVC Main at native 4K and 30 fps, with a 5 Mbps target and rate
limit. Capture follows the device orientation at startup: 3840×2160 landscape
or 2160×3840 portrait. Live viewing retains its negotiated H.264 resolution,
up to 1080p. Five RTP stream slots allow four viewers plus the hub. Viewers
share one capture input and AAC-ELD microphone encoder; viewers matching the
capture owner's video settings also share its H.264 encoder. Closing one viewer preserves the others and
recording. Recording audio is mono 16kHz at 16 kbps.

The MP4 writer packages the hardware encoder's HEVC configuration and producer
reference timestamps, preserves fractional-frame timing and audio alignment,
and sends a four-second decodable prebuffer. Camera Off stops capture and
refuses cached snapshots. Disarming recording preserves live viewing; disabling
Record Audio discards buffered and subsequent microphone samples.

## Compatibility

Tested Home hubs saved native 4K HEVC through the existing encrypted HomeKit Data
Stream transport while negotiating the legacy 1080p H.264 configuration. The
new HKSV service profile and 4K live streaming are not implemented. Compatibility
with other hub/client combinations needs testing. No new dependency is added.
[Scrypted's recording code](https://github.com/koush/scrypted/blob/main/plugins/homekit/src/types/camera/camera-recording.ts)
provided the reference for HEVC producer timestamps over HDS.

## Verification

On 2026-10-12, real hand motion and claps produced saved Home exports on an iPad
Pro M4 and iPhone 18 Pro Max running OS 27.0.1, using a HomePod mini on OS 27.
FFprobe confirmed native 3840×2160 HEVC Main at 30 fps. Native audio decoding
confirmed real microphone sound; full video decoding passed.

| Source/test | Video sample duration | Audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iPad, real motion | 16 s and 12 s | None |
| iPad, recording during live view | 24 s | AAC-ELD, previous 24 kbps setting |
| iPad, idle recording | 20 s | AAC-ELD, about 15.8 kbps |
| iPhone, clean Release build | 16 s / 480 frames | AAC-ELD, about 16 kbps |
| iPad, final shared-capture build | 16 s / 480 frames | Non-silent AAC-ELD, 15.69 s |

The iPhone export has a four-second timeline offset, so its 20-second endpoint
is not its playback duration. Both recording and live preview, persisted 4K
settings, camera Off, recording disarm/re-arm, and audio off/on were exercised.
An iPhone 15 was unavailable for physical testing.

All eight packages passed in Release (436 tests). All 101 app tests, with 102
parameterized executions per platform, passed on Mac and the physical iPad
and iPhone. Tests cover four separately encrypted viewers, mixed
video settings, audio sharing, session isolation, and privacy controls. Native
portrait and landscape HEVC encode/MP4/decode tests passed; physical portrait
recording was not tested. Mac and iPhone Home displayed the iPad simultaneously;
the iPhone remained live after the Mac viewer closed. Four physical viewers
were not tested. Strict formatting and signed Release builds passed. Private footage, device IDs,
signing credentials, and temporary diagnostics are excluded from the repo.

Stop Device Hub screen sharing before testing camera capture; it produced black
frames on the test iPad. Home recordings can take several minutes to appear.
HDS acknowledgements alone do not prove that Home saved a clip.
